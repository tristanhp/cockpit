# S5 — Le cap and la clôture

Goal: the two daily rituals run in the app on prefilled screens. Depends on S2 and S3. Product rules: [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) (the four moments, goals and habits).

## 1. Data
- `habits`: id, name, `targetPct` (default 80), `protected` (boolean, never traded away), `active`, sort order. Config-driven: `config/seed.*.json` gets an optional `habits` array; the demo database has generic ones (reading, stretching, sleep before midnight…). A new habit never needs code.
- `habit_logs`: habit id, day, done.
- `day_plans`: day, `firstActionTaskId`, `top3` (json of task ids), `closedAt` (nullable), `snapshot` (json taken at closing: task ids and due dates, event ids and times, used to show what changed overnight).
- Settings: cap times (weekdays and weekends), clôture time (default 22:45), hard stop (23:00).

## 2. La clôture (`/cloture`)
A five-step sequence with a progress indicator, each step prefilled so most take one tap. Pure helpers (`src/engine/cloture.ts`, unit-tested) compute the suggestions.
1. **Bilan**: blocks kept out of planned, tasks done, effort points done. Read only.
2. **Restes**: everything in À replacer and late. For each: *demain*, *plus tard cette semaine* (Cockpit chooses, using S3), *abandonner*. Applies through the S3 replan code.
3. **Inbox**: today's captures with suggested destination and date; confirm or change; "Tout ranger".
4. **Demain**: three suggested priorities (Urgent and Haute work and due dates, never more than three), today's habits to tick (protected habits first), and one **première action** to start with.
5. **Rien d'oublié ?**: a capture field for anything that came up today (same parser as S2). Then the closing screen: "Écrans off. Le carnet papier t'attend." with a quiet dark layout and no further actions.
Closing stores the `snapshot`, sets `closedAt` and records the habit logs. After 23:00 the sequence still works but shows a gentle "il est tard" notice. Reachable from a button on Aujourd'hui after 22:30, from the palette and from the S4 notification: the existing 22:45 and morning-cap notifications in `src/engine/notifications.ts` must open `/cloture` and Aujourd'hui, and read their times from the new cap and clôture settings.

## 3. Le cap
- A top card on Aujourd'hui from the cap time until you press *C'est parti* (stored per day): the première action, the three priorities with their blocks, and **Depuis hier soir** (diff against the snapshot: new or changed tickets, moved events, new clashes between blocks and events).
- If yesterday had no `closedAt`, show a one-minute **catch-up** above the card: leftovers with *demain* / *plus tard* / *abandonner* and the inbox, then continue.
- *Ajuster* lets you reorder the three priorities and swap the première action; changes update the day plan.
- *C'est parti* starts the first block (marks it in progress; S3's Maintenant strip takes over).

## 4. Habits
- A habits section in the clôture (step 4) and a small `/habitudes` page: weekly percentage against the target, a plain row of seven days, and the "jamais deux fois de suite" rule shown as a quiet note when a habit missed two weeks in a row (computed, unit-tested). No streak counters, no shaming; 80 % is a full week.

## Done when
- In demo mode: the full clôture runs end to end and writes the day plan, habit logs and snapshot; the next morning's cap (simulated by setting the clock through a `COCKPIT_NOW` env override used only in demo and tests) shows the overnight changes and the catch-up when a clôture was skipped.
- Unit tests cover the suggestions (three priorities, première action), the snapshot diff and the habit weekly percentage. `npm run check` passes.
